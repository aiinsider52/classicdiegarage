# Design System

## Theme

Black cinematic future-tech stage with iridescent violet, blue, and emerald AI forms. Premium SaaS presentation with restrained product panels.

## Typography

- Display and body: Manrope
- Display accent: Georgia italic

## Color

- Background: near-black neutral
- Surfaces: deep blue-black
- AI primary: royal violet
- AI secondary: electric blue
- Verified result: signal green
- Text: cool white and blue-gray

## Layout

Centered cinematic hero, one decisive AI visual, restrained SaaS product window, soft panels, generous section spacing.

## Motion

Slow orchestration pulses, data-flow movement, and purposeful entrance choreography. Reduced-motion fallback required.
