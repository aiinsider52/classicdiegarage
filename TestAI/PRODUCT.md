# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Owners, CEOs, COOs, and operations leaders of established European businesses evaluating practical AI adoption.

## Product Purpose

Position AI Insider as a serious enterprise AI implementation partner. Convert qualified visitors into discovery calls by showing measurable business outcomes, production capability, and responsible governance.

## Brand Personality

Precise, authoritative, advanced.

## Anti-references

Generic AI agencies, playful startup templates, glowing brain imagery, purple gradient blobs, vague claims without proof, and self-serve SaaS positioning.

## Design Principles

- Lead with business economics, not technology hype.
- Show AI as controlled operating infrastructure.
- Make every major claim measurable.
- Signal enterprise readiness through governance and process.
- Keep complexity visible but understandable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard navigation, readable contrast, responsive layouts, and reduced motion.
